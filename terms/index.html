Terms for the catalogue

These terms cover pages under crinaro.ai/catalog/ only. They do not cover any other part of crinaro.ai.

Our reading, not the vendor's

Each line in the catalogue is our summary of one page published by a product's maker or by its documentation host, as we read that page on the date shown beside the line. It is an opinion, offered to help a person or a team with a decision, and the reader is expected to confirm it on the vendor's page. The vendor's page is the authority on what the product does and on the vendor's own terms. A line is not a statement by the vendor.

The catalogue is not legal, compliance, security or procurement advice.

Where we found nothing on a page we say so by listing the pages we read. That means we did not find it on those pages. It never means the vendor does not offer it.

Corrections

To report an error or ask for a correction, write to report@crinaro.ai . Send the page you are looking at, the line you think is wrong, and the vendor's page that shows it. The address is read by people. We use what you send to correct the catalogue and to improve how it is made, and we do not publish your message or your name. A correction is made once we have confirmed it against the vendor's page, and the corrected line carries its new date. We make no promise about how quickly we reply.

How the catalogue is made

The catalogue was produced with model assistance under human direction and review. A language model drafts the summary of a page, and a reviewer reads the wording. Each line shows whether a second reviewer has read its wording. A line is a summary of the page in the catalogue's words, which may follow the page's wording closely, and the page is the authority.

Every line links the page it summarizes and shows the date we read it. No line copies a run of a vendor's words, and the build refuses a line that does.

This build was made on 2026-10-21 from 29 line(s) about 18 product(s). 68 reading(s) were left out for want of a second read, an age past 90 days, or a check.

Dates and expiry

Every line shows the date we read its page, and a page may have changed since. A line is built with a 90-day limit: a line whose page was read more than 90 days before a build is left out of that build.
