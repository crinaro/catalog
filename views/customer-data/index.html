Customer data, by cloud and model

What each cloud's own pages say happens to your prompts and outputs when you call a model there: where they are processed, whether they are retained or reviewed, and whether the model's maker can see them, for each model, routing tier and region the pages were read for. Three clouds are covered: Amazon Bedrock, Microsoft Foundry and Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. Calling a model directly from its maker is not covered here. This covers the model call only, not what a tool that calls the model does with your data; that is on each product's page. Nothing here is a compliance determination.

A summary is not the company's wording and not a statement by that company, except that it may give the company's exact name for a product, plan or option in quotation marks, with the company named in the same sentence. Someone other than its writer checked it against the passage. The date beside each line is the date of the read that line rests on. The exact wording was on the linked page, in the section named, on the date beside the line; the page may have changed since, so read it there before a decision turns on it. A line about a table cell that holds only a mark, or nothing, is generated from the cell rather than written by hand: it says whether the table marks that row and column, excludes it, sends it to a sales conversation or leaves it empty, and gives the cell's mark. Such a line is labeled "From the cell:" rather than "Summary:". A cell that holds words is summarized and checked like a sentence. A line with no label summarizes a sentence the page states. A labeled line reads excluded on the maker's page (the page excludes the pair), sent to a sales conversation (the page sends the question to a sales conversation), left blank in the maker's table (the pair's cell in the cloud's own table is empty, and this page reads an empty cell as neither answer), pages disagree (two sentences equally specific to the pair are recorded as disagreeing: one excludes what the other states, or the disagreement itself is recorded; both are shown; equally specific sentences not recorded as disagreeing are shown as "also, equally specific", and this page does not say whether they agree), NOT-LISTED (the cloud's own table for that model has no row or column for the pair, or a page read for the pair is recorded as listing it nowhere, and the line says which), not stated on the pages read (no sentence bound to the pair has been recorded from the pages listed, which says nothing about any contract) or page could not be read (a page for that cloud returned nothing readable). A pair whose availability reads excluded on the maker's page, left blank in the maker's table, NOT-LISTED or not stated on the pages read is shown so its other lines can be read; none of them says the pair is offered. Where several sentences apply, the one recorded as binding the pair most narrowly is shown first and the broader ones under it; where that first line excludes the pair or is blank, the broader ones are context and not the pair's answer. Binding is this page's record of which pairs a sentence covers, and a sentence can be bound to a model or tier it does not name. A part of a pair's heading that this page records no value for, or that no line under the pair is bound to, reads "no region recorded here" or the like.
