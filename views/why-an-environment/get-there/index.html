You do not have to move everything at once, and you should not stop people discovering on their laptops. This is a path we suggest, not the only one. Move work into the environment once other people rely on it, once it runs on a schedule, or once it touches customer data.

  1. Keep discovery on laptops, without customer data. Decide which data may go into a personal AI assistant at all, and say plainly that customer data is not on the list. This is what reaches the service rep and the seller, whose tools sit outside the environment. Locking down production data in step 4 does not stop a person pasting what is already on their screen.
  2. Choose the AI services, through your own account. For each kind of data, decide the model, where it is processed and how long it is kept. Customer data, by cloud and model shows what each cloud's own pages say.
  3. Start with sharing. Give people one place where anyone authorized can upload pages, name them and choose who sees them, shared as they were built. It is a simple first use, and the one we expect to bring in people outside engineering. In our experience, when individuals share, that starts reuse.
  4. Lock down production data, so AI work reaches it only from inside. This is the step that changes how people work. A support engineer who copies records to a laptop today needs a way to do the same work inside the environment, so plan that before you close the path. The closed path holds only once this access is actually removed. We have not measured what this step costs.
  5. Move the shared work in. A report moves with its owner, co-owners and audience. An AI team that refreshes it moves too, with its passwords and keys kept in the environment rather than in a file next to the app. The copy in the laptop file stays until someone deletes it.
  6. Connect more data, then give people workspaces, for engineers and for people outside engineering.

What this means for you

Steps 1 and 2 are decisions rather than builds. Step 3 is the first visible gain. Step 4 changes how people work and its cost is unmeasured, so plan it deliberately.
