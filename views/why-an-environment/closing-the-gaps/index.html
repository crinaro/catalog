The gaps this walkthrough showed

Four gaps ran through the earlier pages:

  • Work stops with the person who made it. In our experience, what one person builds with an assistant reaches colleagues as a picture, so nobody builds on it.
  • Customer data reaches services nobody chose for it. The assistant sends it wherever it is set up to send it, an approved service included.
  • Passwords and keys sit in files on laptops. In our experience they usually sit in a plain text file next to the app, and travel wherever the folder goes.
  • One question is open: whose permissions an unattended run uses, when an AI team refreshes a report with nobody present.

What no catalog product fills today

The requirements published so far for an environment aimed at these gaps are in this catalog, with the cloud services and products that can fill each part: Governed engineering platform: requirements and options. The data gap closes only once laptops lose direct access to production data, and the fourth gap is there as an open question. Some parts have no catalog product recorded for them. On that page they are the rows marked "Built by the organization", each with the reason. Those are the parts your own teams would have to make.
