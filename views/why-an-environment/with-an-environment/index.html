An environment for AI work gives the work people do inside it what your products already have. Laptops stay a place for discovery, outside the environment.

An AI team is a set of AI programs set up to work together on one job, such as refreshing a report.

Today and with an environment

  • Where customer data goes. Today: wherever each person's assistant sends it. With an environment: production data is locked down, so AI work on it happens inside, where only the AI services your company approved are reachable, once laptops lose direct access to that data. An assistant on a person's own laptop is outside the environment, so these rules do not reach it.
  • How a page is shared. Today: as a picture in slides or an email, seen by whoever received it and whoever they forward it to. With an environment: as it was built, so a viewer can still drill into the data, seen only by the people or groups its owner chose, though a viewer's own assistant can still read it from their screen.
  • A report that keeps itself current. Today: it runs on one laptop, while that laptop is on. With an environment: an AI team refreshes it on a schedule, with a named owner and co-owners.
  • What the AI can reach. Today: whatever the person can reach from their own machine. With an environment: each request is checked against who is asking, so a data source that accepts that person's identity returns only what that person may already see.
  • When someone leaves. Today: their reports and helpers leave with their laptop. With an environment: what they own is handed on.

One question is still open: when an AI team runs on a schedule with nobody present, whose permissions it runs with.

What this means for you

You get the three benefits ranked on page 2, the same for engineers and for people outside engineering: protected data is critical, and in our experience sharing, reuse and speed is the bigger prize.
