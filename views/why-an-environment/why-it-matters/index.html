Each person's laptop running an AI assistant is now a place like the ones you already control. It reaches company data, calls outside services and produces work others rely on, with no shared rules around it.

Four terms. A model is the AI system that writes the answers. An AI service is an online service that runs a model for you. A workstation is the laptop or desktop a person works on. Production data is the live data your customers' records are held in.

A laptop is good for discovery and stops there

Keep it. People learn fast on their own machines. Three things come after discovery, and a laptop does none of them:

  • Reuse. A useful report, page or AI helper stays on the machine that made it. Nobody else can find it or build on it.
  • Scaling. Each person gets faster, but there is no shared place for what they build, so the gains do not add up across the team.
  • Protecting the data that is critical to you. The assistant sends customer data wherever it is set up to send it, which may be a service your contracts, auditors or customers would not accept.

What an environment gives you, ranked

An environment for AI work gives engineers and people outside engineering three things. We rank them. Protecting the data is critical. In our experience the bigger prize is sharing, reuse and speed, because reuse starts when individuals share what they build.

  • Faster reuse. A page or helper shared as it was built can be opened, explored and built on by the next person.
  • Protected data. The company chooses which AI services take customer data in, and AI work on production data happens inside the environment, once laptops lose direct access to that data.
  • Compliance where the work runs. The rules on which services may hold customer data are enforced inside the environment rather than left to each assistant. Whether a given setup meets a contract or a regulation is for your company and its auditors to decide.

What this means for you

  • If you answer for customers, contracts or compliance: you can say which AI services customer data could reach, for work inside the environment, once workstation access to that data is removed.
  • If you run a team: what one person builds has somewhere to live, so the speed adds up.
  • If you do the work: a good report or helper you make can be used by others, with your name on it.
