One governed place for AI-built work

This is what we are building. It is not available today.

The aim is one governed place where AI-built work is shared as built, and customer data goes only where you chose. A company using it would have:

  • Sign-in with the company login. People use the Microsoft or Google account they already have.
  • Sharing as built. People upload pages and small apps and choose who can open them, by name, or by your identity provider's groups when it runs in your own cloud, so a viewer can still click, filter and drill.
  • Passwords and keys in a vault. An app's secrets are kept in a protected store, where each app reads only its own, rather than in a file on a laptop.
  • AI work through the services you approved. AI work reaches only the AI services your company chose, through its own cloud account.
  • Workspaces. Engineers and people outside engineering work in the same environment.
  • Data reached by each person's own access, where the data source supports it.
  • Visibility for administrators. They can see who can open what, with a log of the decisions.

We plan to offer it two ways. Hosted by us is the main one: pages are kept in our storage, one tenant per company, so decide which data may go onto them, as step 1 does for AI assistants. In your own cloud is the option for a company that wants the same pieces inside its own engineering platform; there, your teams still set up DNS, a certificate, a sign-in registration and routing.
