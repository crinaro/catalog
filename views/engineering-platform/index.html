Governed engineering platform: requirements and options

A reference pattern for a governed engineering platform: the requirements it has to meet, numbered, and for each part of the platform the cloud services, catalog products and parts built in-house that can fill it.

Use this page to look things up. A list of requirements says what a platform has to do; it does not say how to build one, in what order, or whether to.

Nothing on this page has had its second read. Each requirement and option appears here once someone other than its writer has read it.

A requirement or option no second reader has read, a cloud service with no recorded source, and a catalog product whose page carries no line a second reader has read are held back from this page until they have one.

"Reported in use" marks an option one build used, as that build was reported to us. The report is testimony: we did not observe the build, and the page names no organization or person.

Each product links its catalog page, which carries the readings behind it. This page adds no reading of its own about any product.
