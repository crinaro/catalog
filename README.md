# Crinaro catalog

Our reading of what vendors' own pages say about their products, as of the dates shown, for a reader to confirm on the vendor's page, and on some product pages our own readings, labeled as such. This repository holds the generated output only; it is built elsewhere and is not edited by hand.

No record shows a person reading any item, and some setup links and requirement phrases are published with no reviewer having read them. The terms carry the provenance, the warranty and the liability wording, in one place.
